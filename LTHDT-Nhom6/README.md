# He thong quan ly mua ve xem phim

Ung dung Java Swing thuần, khong can React, Maven hoac thu vien ben ngoai.

## Chay ung dung

- Cai JDK 17 tro len.
- Tren Windows, chay `run.bat`.
- Hoac bien dich cac file trong `src` vao thu muc `out`, sau do chay `cinema.main.Main`.

Lan dau mo ung dung, danh sach khach hang va ve trong. Nhap thong tin khach hang o tab **Khach hang** truoc khi dat ve. Phim va suat chieu mau duoc tao de co the thu nghiem ngay.

Thong tin khach hang va ve duoc luu trong thu muc `data` ben canh ung dung. Phim va suat chieu hien duoc khai bao mau trong `CinemaService`.

## Chuc nang

- Them khach hang, kiem tra du lieu va khong cho trung so dien thoai.
- Dat ghe theo suat chieu, tu dong an ghe da dat.
- Tinh phu phi ghe doi/VIP va uu dai sinh vien/thanh vien VIP.
- Thanh toan bang tien mat, chuyen khoan hoac vi dien tu.
- Luu khach hang va ve qua file, tu nap lai khi mo ung dung.
